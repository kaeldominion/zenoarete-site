# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective guests, retreat organisers, creative teams, athletes, and groups comparing premium private villas in Bali. They need to understand the scale, character, sleeping arrangements, wellness facilities, service level, and booking options quickly on mobile or desktop.

## Product Purpose

Present Villa Zeno Arete as a distinctive six-suite private sanctuary in Pererenan. The site should make every suite and shared area easy to explore, support confident booking through Airbnb, direct booking, or Nusa Nova WhatsApp, and preserve the villa's philosophy-led identity without compromising usability or speed.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, mythic, and immersive. The tone combines physical strength, intentional recovery, strategic intelligence, and quiet luxury. The villa should feel designed for both celebration and self-mastery.

## Anti-references

- Generic beige luxury-villa templates.
- A visual copy of Villa Seger or the Nusa Nova listing interface.
- Aggressive gym branding that overwhelms hospitality and comfort.
- Empty marketing claims without photographic evidence.
- Hidden content, slow galleries, or decorative effects that make the property harder to explore.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the black, gold, and architectural identity already associated with Zeno Arete.
2. Let current property photography prove every claim.
3. Organise the experience around six named suites and clearly defined shared spaces.
4. Balance discipline and intensity with comfort, privacy, and recovery.
5. Keep booking routes visible without interrupting the property story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction standards. Maintain keyboard-accessible galleries and booking controls, useful alternative text, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and content that remains visible if animation or client-side JavaScript is interrupted.
